# Singularity Studies

> **From prediction to observation.**

We study the Singularity not as a date to predict, but as a transition to observe.

This organization is building an open research program and infrastructure for
Singularity Studies. Its empirical program examines transitions in human
criticality, improvement loops, sociotechnical organization, and lived human
change.

## Academic architecture

```text
Singularity Studies
└── Empirical Singularity Studies
    ├── Observational Singularity Studies (シンギュラリティ観測学)
    └── Anthropology of the Singularity (シンギュラリティ人類学)
```

**Empirical Singularity Studies has two eyes. Observational Singularity Studies
looks at technology. Anthropology of the Singularity looks at humanity.**

### Observational Singularity Studies

> *What is becoming possible without us?*

- **Japanese:** シンギュラリティ観測学
- **Perspective:** technology and sociotechnical systems
- **Instrument:** Singularity Observatory
- **Method:** cross-domain longitudinal observation
- **Repository:** [singularity-studies/observatory](https://github.com/singularity-studies/observatory)

This repository develops infrastructure for system-side evidence and
longitudinal transition observation.

### Anthropology of the Singularity

> *What are we becoming alongside it?*

- **Japanese:** シンギュラリティ人類学
- **Perspective:** humanity and lived transition
- **Instruments:** field notes and life histories
- **Method:** longitudinal ethnography
- **Repository:** [singularity-studies/anthropology](https://github.com/singularity-studies/anthropology)

This repository develops infrastructure for human-side longitudinal
ethnography and the study of lived transition.

## Scientific stance

The program does not assume that the Singularity will occur or that human
criticality is necessarily disappearing. Persistence, reversal, human
re-entry, resistance, non-adoption, and re-humanization are legitimate
empirical outcomes. Current AI systems are not treated as evidence that the
Singularity has occurred, and AGI/ASI benchmark ladders do not define the
field's empirical framework.

The two repositories are complementary but scientifically independent.
Neither repository authorizes the other.
